What a complete plan still needs

Beyond the missing component pages, a full engineering plan at early-design level needs these system-level documents. None exist yet:

  • Requirements, each numbered and traced from the mission objective down to each component.
  • Concept of operations, describing a day, a lunar night and an emergency at the base.
  • Base-wide budgets for mass, power and heat.
  • Launch manifest, listing which hardware flies on which lander, and when.
  • Integrated schedule with the critical path.
  • Interface control documents between systems.
  • Bills of materials for every component.
  • Risk register scored by likelihood and consequence.
  • Trade studies, for example fission vs solar-only, or molten electrolysis vs carbothermal.
  • Verification matrix, showing how each requirement is proven.
