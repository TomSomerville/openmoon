## 1. Only technology that exists or is close

Every system must be one of the following:

- flying today
- built and tested on the ground
- in active development with a credible path to flight within **3–5 years**

We use NASA's **Technology Readiness Level** (TRL) scale from 1 (idea) to 9 (flight-proven)<sup class="r">[1](#r1)</sup>, and show it on every technology. Anything below TRL 6 is marked **develop**, and the plan says what work closes the gap.

No warp drives, fusion reactors, space elevators, or anything else that needs physics or materials we don't have.

## 2. Every fact has a source

Every number links to where it came from. Sources are preferred in this order:

1. official documentation: NASA, ESA, JAXA, NASA's Inspector General, GAO, NTRS technical reports, company releases about their own hardware
2. encyclopaedias and wikis
3. code and data repositories

Reputable space press is used only where no primary source exists, and is labelled as secondary.

## 3. Estimates are labelled

Anything this plan calculates rather than cites is marked **ESTIMATE**, with the basis shown. Costs are planning numbers, not quotes. Every cost line says how it was built.

## 4. Every problem gets a solution, or is marked open

Each milestone and component lists what can go wrong (failure modes), how bad it is, and how the plan deals with it. Where there is no good answer yet, the page says so.

## 5. Self-sustaining means by mass

No colony built with today's technology will make its own microchips or medicines by 2050. Open Moon measures self-sufficiency as the **share of mass**, consumables and construction, made on the Moon.

## 6. Buy before build

Where a service exists or is being built (launch, landers, relays, rovers), Open Moon buys it rather than duplicating it. Its own engineering effort goes where nobody else is working.

## 7. Open source

Everything is published under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Anyone can copy, adapt and improve the plan, as long as they credit it and share alike. Contributions are welcome through [GitHub]({{ site.repo }}).
