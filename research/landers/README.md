## What this means for site selection

- **No lander publishes a certified slope above 10°.** The only design figures are Nova-C's 10° and Griffin's "easily tolerate 10°". Landing-site rules from Astrobotic, Firefly's team and NASA's crewed landers are all ≤10°.
- **The one landing on steeper ground failed.** IM-1 touched down on 12° terrain (and too fast) and tipped over.
- **NASA's crewed landers must also end up tilted no more than 8°.**
- Open Moon's maps currently mark ≤15° as landing-suitable. The evidence above supports ≤10° for robotic landers in M01–M05. Changing the threshold is an open decision.
