Fission surface power unit

Problems and solutions 4

Critical

Lunar night lasts about 14 Earth days

Solar-only outposts must store two weeks of energy. Solution: the reactor provides base load independent of sunlight; batteries cover only peaks.

High

Abrasive dust on radiators

Dust lowers radiator emissivity. Solution: mount radiators vertically, place the unit away from landing pads, and test electrodynamic dust shields.

High

Radiation dose at the outpost

Solution: site the reactor at a set distance behind a regolith berm, so the terrain adds shielding.

Medium

Launch safety and public approval

Solution: launch with fresh, unirradiated fuel and keep the reactor subcritical until it is on the surface.
