Open Moon: choose a design

Five candidate templates. Each shows the same sample page, Phase 1 → Power → Fission surface power unit, so you can compare them directly.

01

Dossier

Dark technical documentation. A plan tree on the left, spec tables and colour-coded problem cards.
02

Blueprint

Engineering drawing sheet. Numbered callouts on a schematic, failure modes and a title block.
03

Whitepaper

Long-form editorial. Serif chapters, margin notes and numbered references. Follows light or dark mode.
04

Mission Control

Dashboard. Phase timeline, TRL meters per component, phase gate checklist and top risks.
05

Open Wiki

Contributor-first wiki. Searchable collapsible tree, tabs, and problems as open, solved or needs-research issues.
